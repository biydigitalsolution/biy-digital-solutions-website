FAST CLIENT CUSTOMIZATION CHECKLIST
1. Replace the business name "Evergreen Service Co." in index.html.
2. Replace the letter E inside the brand-mark with the client initials, or replace the brand area with the client logo.
3. Replace assets/hero-photo.jpg with the client hero photo.
4. Replace assets/about-photo.jpg with the owner or team photo.
5. Update the three service cards.
6. Update phone, email, city, reviews, statistics, and footer copyright.
7. Change the colors at the top of styles.css under :root.
8. Connect the contact form to Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, a booking page, or the client’s preferred form service.
9. Keep the Return to BIY Digital Solutions link for the public demo. Remove it before delivering the client’s production site.
